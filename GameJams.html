Game Jams

Released on itch.io in 2018, 2022, 2023, and 2026

Semi-regularly participate in Ludum Dare and GMTK game jams. Created games with a small team size of 4-7 people. Games are made based on a selected theme and created entirely in 2-4 days depending on the Jam. Out of 6 projects, 5 have been ranked in the top 1-10% in at least one category from thousands of entries. I like mentioning that VRn't ranked 17th in humor. Crapshot Saloon and FedUp had post-jam versions made where we further polished the games and added additional features and modes.
